# Line 4 (D6) measured: end of turn to `hint.start`

Mac mini M4 (awake, `pmset sleep 0`), whisper.cpp `small`, llama.cpp gemma-4-E2B for the
hints, through the `ssh -L` tunnels; 150 s of the 2026-11-15 call (`--start 150 --end 300`,
`--end-windows 24`, `--bleed-ratio 2.0`), real hints (AUDIT D8 gates off). Three pairs,
alternating the core from before D6 (`aecb8c9`) and after it (`2da75e5`), same
`bench/replay_call.py` for both (it feeds through `admit` plus a worker when the core has
one, in line when it has not). "End of turn to `hint.start`" is measured from the audio
frame at which the far lane's turn end was noticed, so it holds the detector's delay, the
recognition backlog and the flush. Raw JSON stays local (`sessions/` is git-ignored).

| | before D6 | after D6 |
|---|---|---|
| end of turn to `hint.start`, median per run, s | 0.60, 0.63, 0.65 | 0.60, 0.63, 0.61 |
| slowest of 9 hints, s | 1.88, 1.87, 2.35 | 5.45, 1.61, 1.82 |
| speculative hints started (per run) | 9, 9, 9 | 9, 9, 9 |
| cancelled (wasted) | 2, 2, 2 | 0, 0, 0 |
| TTFT median, ms | 323, 328, 332 | 322, 303, 307 |
| lag of finals, mean / max, s | 0.8 / 1.9, 0.8 / 1.9, 0.9 / 2.3 | 1.1 / 5.5, 0.8 / 2.0, 0.9 / 2.1 |
| word error far / mic | 0.115 / 0.073 | 0.115 / 0.073 |

- **D6 does not move the median.** As expected from the design: the final of a turn is
  emitted before a hint is built, so `hint.start` waits for the decode ahead of it either
  way. What the change removes is the feeder blocking behind recognition (worst backlog
  3.1-4.2 s on the far lane before, 0 after: that number changed meaning, since the
  feeder no longer awaits recognition) and a stale detector.
- **Wasted generations: 2 of 9 before, 0 of 9 after, in all three runs.** The direction is
  consistent but the cause is not shown: the change cancels a speculative hint *sooner*
  when the other party resumes, which should cancel more, not fewer. A guess is that the
  earlier detector lets a hint finish before the speaker resumes, or that the two
  replays differ in when hints land; nothing here tests either. One call, 9 turns.
- One slow run after D6 (new 1: 5.45 s for one hint, final lag max 5.5 s) did not repeat in
  the other two; cause not investigated (the Mac was in normal use).
- Words and terms are identical before and after: the change does not touch recognition.
- Not measured: the D8 gates (need more calls), D11 on real audio (no 12 s unbroken speech
  in this clip).
