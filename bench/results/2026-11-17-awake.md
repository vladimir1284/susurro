# M0 rerun with the Mac awake — Mac mini M4 16 GB — morning of 2026-11-17

7 rounds (08:50 to 11:41), driven by `bench/overnight.py` with `pmset sleep 0` set on the
Mac (see the sleep finding in [2026-11-17-overnight.md](2026-11-17-overnight.md)). Same
setup as the night run: llama.cpp b10240 and Ollama's own server, ctx 8192, through an
`ssh -L` tunnel. Full tables: [2026-11-17-awake-report.md](2026-11-17-awake-report.md).
Medians across rounds. The Mac was in normal use (browser, Stats app), not idle.

Rounds 5-7 ran back to back instead of 45 min apart (a driver bug, fixed in `b07abd0`),
and rounds 6-7 only did speech and replay. So the language-model rows below have n=5
(Ollama n=2), not 7.

## What changed against the night run

| | night (Mac sleeping ~13 s/min) | awake |
|---|---|---|
| sampler gaps over 14 s | 388 (about 17% of the time) | **0** in 1019 samples |
| failed steps | hung model loads, stalls | **0 of 43** |
| slow outliers | 39 | **0** |
| replay, lag of finals (mean / max, median of rounds) | 15.8 s max without hints | **0.8 s / 1.8 s** (worst round 3.9 s) |
| whisper.cpp real-time factor | 0.050 | 0.053 (peak 0.105) |

The language-model numbers barely moved, which says the sleeps hit the stalls and the lag,
not the steady speed of the models.

## Language model (the `warm_ttft_s` gate is < 1.0 s)

| runtime / model | warm TTFT s | first hint, cold | first hint, after prefill | decode tok/s | server RSS GB |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| llama.cpp gemma-4-E2B | **0.13** | 2.42 | 0.41 | 55 | not measured (see below) |
| llama.cpp LFM2.5-1.2B | **0.41** | 1.27 | 0.20 | 115 | 0.8 |
| llama.cpp LFM2 2.6B | **1.02** | 3.21 | 0.51 | 51 | 1.8 |
| Ollama LFM2.5-1.2B | 0.72 | 1.27 | 0.92 | 112 | 1.1 |
| Ollama LFM2 2.6B | 1.89 | 3.20 | 2.25 | 47 | 0.5 |

- Same conclusions as the night run: gemma-4-E2B and LFM2.5-1.2B pass the gate under
  llama.cpp; LFM2 2.6B does not (1.01-1.02 s in every round); the prefill works under
  llama.cpp (first hint 5-6x faster than cold) and barely under Ollama.
- gemma's server was already running (the user's, not started by the driver), so its
  resident memory reading (0.13 GB) is not the model's and is not used. The 2.7 GB of the
  night run is the figure to keep.
- LFM2 2.6B loaded in all 5 llama.cpp rounds. The hung load of the night run did not
  recur; the cause was never established, so this is "not reproduced", not "fixed".

## Replay with real hints (150 s of the 2026-11-15 call)

- Word error identical every round: far 0.115, mic 0.073.
- 9 hints started, 2 wasted, every round, with gemma and with LFM2.5.
- Hint time to first token while the speech model is busy: 318 ms (gemma), 309 ms (LFM2.5).
- Lag of finals: mean 0.8-0.9 s, max 1.8 s (range 1.6-2.4 s; 1.7-3.9 s over the 7 plain
  replays). No stalls. One clip pair of one call; not a 60-minute call.

## What this says about M0

- The three gate conditions hold for gemma-4-E2B and LFM2.5-1.2B under llama.cpp, now
  without the sleep distortion.
- Still not measured: memory with both models loaded, speech on more than one clip, MLX,
  hint quality, a 60-minute call. The Mac still varies about 2x between rounds (whisper
  real-time factor 0.047 to 0.105) with no sleeping, so keep reporting medians.
